## Version 9 publication — first-player feedback pass

**Skybound Test version 9 is published** to place `86507299893768` / universe `10770074500`, from source **`072782f`**. Studio confirmed "Place published. Playtesters can now play this place" at **2026-10-11T00:19:48Z** (17:19:48 PDT, October 10). Version 8, from `8920699`, went out at 2026-10-10T23:42:52Z with the HUD changes only. Access settings were not touched.

A first unfamiliar player (a child, on a phone) said it was hard to tell what was being presented and that there seemed to be only three skills. One unassisted bot match on version 7 agreed: the fight pill read "BATTLE!" almost throughout, each pick showed three of nine skills, and two of three rounds ran the full 35 seconds to a replayed draw. Versions 8 and 9 respond to that:

- **Fight HUD:** a play-by-play pill (hits, misses, blocks, wild cards, who is winding up), a charge meter in each pilot's attack pill, wild-card chips that flash when they fire, and a round card with hit counts held for 3.5 seconds.
- **Draft:** every pick shows the whole eligible roster in a fixed order; Reroll and Pick Any are off; the pick clock is 15 seconds; card text is reworded for young players.
- **Tempo:** armor decides a round at 22 seconds, level armor goes to sudden death (next armor lost ends it), 35 seconds remains the hard stop. Planes line up and bank away faster.
- **Skills:** Magnet pulls during the owner's attack rather than after it.

Five packaged scripts changed (`BattleSimulation`, `BattleSkills`, `SkillCatalog`, client and server `BattleLab`); each was applied to the cloud Edit place and its byte length matched source before publishing. The other thirty were not re-compared in this pass. Python checks passed 41 simulation, 5 catalog and 44 full-server scenarios. Native Studio specs were not rerun.

Seen in Studio play on desktop and an emulated iPhone 14 in landscape: both pick screens, the play-by-play, sudden death, the round card and the match result. A bot match with no input took about 120 seconds for three rounds, 45 of them idle pick clocks. **Not checked:** phone portrait, a physical phone, two human clients, heard audio, and the plane-side words under the new code (the capture tool does not draw them; they were confirmed rendering on version 7). On a landscape phone the REMATCH button still sits partly below the fold of the result screen, as before.

## What still prevents release approval

- **Physical phone:** touch targets, notches/safe areas, frame stability and sustained thermal performance. Small-landscape combat still reads small in sampled frames, especially with reduced motion. The fixed draft is usable in emulation; that does not settle combat readability.
- **Published two-account session:** version 7 is now recorded, but agreement, lifecycle and same-server invitations still need verification with two independent accounts. The local QA pass below the publication updates did not provide that evidence.
- **Live persistence:** confirmed grants/equipment/mastery, leave/rejoin, failure recovery and lease behavior against the actual store.
- **Audio and complete effects review:** hear the mix and published asset permissions; inspect whole-fight camera/knockout sequences and every skill tell. Frame samples and native assertions do not satisfy this. A normal draw screen and the corrected armor-timeout result were not visually reached after the final rebuild.
- **Unfamiliar-player outcomes and balance:** whether players understand their choices/results and voluntarily replay. Existing draw/stall and strong-build flags in [the balance report](BALANCE-REPORT.md) remain. Five bot matches do not prove retention or a balanced roster.
- **Minor presentation polish:** the hangar mastery label currently says “1 MATCHES.” This does not block the loop but should be corrected with the next presentation pass.
